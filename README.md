# Atharva Chourey

**I build audit machinery for measurements a field forgot to check.**

My research programme takes published scientific and historical measurements that everyone cites and nobody re-verifies, and subjects them to independent re-measurement, archival recovery, and pre-registered blind benchmarks.

## Research

- **The Lost Atlas** — an end-to-end audit of a comparative brain archive: 53 of 71 labels recovered with real data, the first MRI hippocampus measurement for the lion (2.02 cm³, against a cited histological 4.50 cm³), and a systematic ~1.6× histology-over-MRI offset across 343 species.
- **Gazetteers and the famine of 1900** — the first statistical extraction from the 1908 Imperial Gazetteers (34 units): princely states lost 16.2% of population vs 7.5% in British districts; rainfall correlation ≈ 0. Political boundaries, not weather.
- **The eclipse-radius archive** — a re-reduction of historical eclipse observations with modern lunar topography: 959.494 ± 0.044″, and a cross-archive discrepancy (7 of 9 overlaps, one probable sign error) that inverts the classical shrinkage claim.
- **Auditing received numbers in Indian history** — bearer-by-bearer checks of widely repeated figures, including Nalanda's enrolment (the 10,000 figure is a later biography's, not Xuanzang's; defensible peak ~3,000–3,500).
- **The Testability Atlas** — of 23 standard questions in Indian historical statistics, only 4 are testable with the data that survives; 11 are underpowered, 8 untestable.

## Method

Pre-registration before extraction · novelty scrub before claiming · blind benchmarks before trusting a pipeline · self-correction on the record.
