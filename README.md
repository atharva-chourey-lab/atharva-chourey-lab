# Atharva Chourey

**Auditing the measurements science stopped checking.**

Independent research in neuroanatomy, astronomy, and historical statistics. One programme: take the published measurements everyone cites and nobody re-verifies, and check them against the archives they came from.

## Research

**Histological brain volumes run 1.6× above MRI volumes** — *Neuroanatomy, 2026*
An end-to-end audit of a public comparative-brain archive recovered 53 usable labels of 71 (the rest were empty placeholders) and produced the first MRI hippocampus measurement for the lion: 2.02 cm³, against the 4.50 cm³ histological value cited since 2007. Across 343 species the histology–MRI offset is systematic at ~1.6×.

**Rainfall did not drive the famine of 1900** — *Historical statistics, 2026*
The first statistical extraction from the 1908 Imperial Gazetteers (34 units): princely states lost 16.2% of population, British districts 7.5%, and the correlation with rainfall is −0.05. Political boundaries, not weather, carry the signal.

**The shrinking-Sun claim rests on a probable sign error** — *Astronomy, 2026*
Re-reducing historical eclipse observations with modern lunar topography: solar radius 959.494 ± 0.044″, consistent with neither Auwers nor the IAU standard. The two canonical reductions disagree on 7 of 9 overlapping eclipses, with five sign flips.

**Nalanda's ten thousand students are a misattribution** — *History of science, 2026*
The figure belongs to a seventh-century biography, not Xuanzang. One of two dozen received numbers audited bearer by bearer; Nalanda's capacity ceiling is below 2,000, its defensible peak near 3,000–3,500.

**Most questions in Indian historical statistics cannot be answered** — *Methodology, 2026*
The Testability Atlas: of 23 standard questions graded against surviving data, 4 are testable, 11 underpowered, 8 untestable in principle.

Pre-registration before extraction · novelty scrub before claiming · blind benchmarks before trusting a pipeline · self-correction on the record.
